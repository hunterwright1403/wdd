Portfolio of Projects I Have Worked On

Hover over the image to see the description.

This is a CAD model of the Philippines Cebu Temple. This is where my wife and I got married. I made it just by looking at reference pictures from our visits there and from images online.
This is a CAD model of a helical gear setup. I used some of the presets on solidworks to create the gear profile. I then used mates so that when one spins so does the other.
This is a CAD model of a stratocaster guitar. I have been playing guitar since I was 15 years old. I made this just from reference pictures online and any meaurements I could find online.
This is a picture of a guitar that I built from scratch. I planed the maple neck and body flat then I glued everything together. I used a bandsaw, router and sander to shape the neck, body, and cavities. I created covers for the internat componants. I bought each of the electronics componants separately and soldered them together based on a wiring diagram I found online.
This is a picture of a fork I created out of stainless steel. I modeled the 2d profile of the fork on solidworks. Then I programmed a CNC wire EDM to cut the profile. After that I cleaned up the edges and bent the fork by hand to give it the curved side profile forks have. (I'm still trying to find a photo of the real fork)
