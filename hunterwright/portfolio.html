This is a model of the Philippines Cebu Temple. This is where my wife and I got married. I made it just by looking at reference pictures from our visits there and from images online.
This is a model of a stratocaster guitar. I have been playing guitar since I was 15 years old. I made this just from reference pictures online and any meaurements I could find online.
This is a helical gear setup. I used some of the presets on solidworks to create the gear profile. I then used mates so that when one spins so does the other.
This is a picture of a guitar that I built from scratch. I used maple wood I got from a wood dealer. I used the planers at work to make the body and neck flat then I glued everything together. I used a router to shape the body and cavities. I created covers for the internat componants. I bought each of the electronics componants separately and soldered them together based on a wiring diagram I found online for dual humbucker pickups. Everything works except the frets need some adjustment.
This is a picture of a fork I created out of stainless steel. I modeled the 2d profile of the fork on solidworks. Then I programmed a CNC wire EDM to cut the profile. After that I cleaned up the edges and bent the fork by hand to give it the curved side profile forks have.
